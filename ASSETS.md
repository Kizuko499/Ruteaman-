## Ikon

- **Set ikon:** Lucide (https://lucide.dev) — lisensi ISC (bebas digunakan, termasuk komersial).
- **Cara pakai:** path SVG Lucide disematkan sebaris (inline) di komponen Blade `resources/views/components/admin-nav-item.blade.php`; tidak ada dependensi JavaScript/tambahan build. Pemetaan ikon per menu ada di `resources/views/admin/layout.blade.php`.
- Ikon yang dipakai: layout-dashboard, school, shield-check, sliders-horizontal, clipboard-list, scroll-text, mail, contact-round, book-open, users, inbox, file-up, palette, list-checks, images, badge-check, user-round-cog, map-pinned, log-out, layout-grid, settings, check, image, map, circle-user-round.
